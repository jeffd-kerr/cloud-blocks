☁️ CLOUD TETRIS ☁️

How to Play

Pieces float UPWARD from the bottom! Create complete horizontal lines before the board fills up.

Controls

← → Move piece left / right

↑ or Z Rotate clockwise

↓ or X Rotate counter-clockwise

SPACE Instant drop upward

📱 Touch Controls:

Use the on-screen buttons to move, rotate, and drop!
